Weather for your stages

Forecast for the highest point and the finish of each stage (on days without a stage, for the town), for daytime hours (08:00–18:00 local time). Mountain and coastal weather can change fast: check again in the morning before you set off.

Weather data: MET Norway (CC BY 4.0); in the Alps and nearby, GeoSphere Austria (CC BY 4.0) for the first days; in the United States, the National Weather Service.
